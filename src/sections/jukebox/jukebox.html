The J is mostly on.

Every 8 to 20 seconds it stutters, three to six times, and now and then it drops to a gas-starved 40 % pink for a few seconds. Under it, a Maestro 160 takes quarters.

The whole night runs on one clock. At a friend’s table everyone sees the same lightning, the same stutter in the J and the same late movie on the corner TV.

In the game, photosensitive mode holds the J steady. So does this page with reduced motion.
