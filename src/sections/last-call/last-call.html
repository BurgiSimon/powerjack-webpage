Before you ask

What will I need?

A computer with a mouse and keyboard, a browser with WebGL 2 and hardware acceleration on, and a window of at least 960 × 540. Single player needs no network once it has loaded.

Can I play on my phone?

This bar needs a mouse. Powerjack is played with a mouse and keyboard: you bend over the table and push the cue with your hand. Come back on a computer and the table will still be here.

Can I play strangers online?

No. Online is friends only, by a six-character table number. No accounts and no matchmaking, and for now you host the table yourself.

When is it out? What will it cost?

There’s no date and no price yet. It’s in development.

Is there drinking and smoking?

It’s a Chicago tavern in 1976, so both are part of the room. Drinks make your aim sway unless the table is sober; coffee and water steady it. Smoking is purely cosmetic: no gameplay buff. Old Jack has an opinion on that last one.

Does it work offline?

Single player does, once it has loaded. It never even loads the network code.

What’s it made of?

About 28,000 lines of TypeScript on three.js, a physics step every millisecond, light in real photometric units, and music synthesised in the browser. The textures and models are CC0, from Poly Haven, ambientCG, Kenney and MakeHuman.

Are these pictures really the game?

Yes. Every picture is a frame the game rendered, or a screenshot of its own menus. The rain on the glass, the smoke around Old Jack and the sound are this page’s own, and the captions say so.
