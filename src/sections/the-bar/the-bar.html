1:52 a.m. · What’ll it be?

Everything on the bar works by hand.

Press E at the bar and your order goes on a guest check. Stan, the bartender you never see, comes out of the kitchen, pours, and a few seconds later your order stands on a coaster. Then it’s in your hands.

None of it is free. Drink adds sway to your stroke; coffee and water steady it. The cigarettes are there because it’s 1976, and they do nothing for your game. At a friend’s table the host can call a sober table: drinks don’t touch anyone’s aim. The tab keeps count.

Chalk
Rub it on with the mouse. How much of the tip you cover sets how far off-centre you can hit before you miscue, and it wears off with every shot.
A glass
Raise it and tilt it to your lips. The surface stays level and the level drops; tip it too steep and you spill or cough.
The lighter
Flick the wheel with a quick mouse motion. It may take a few tries.
