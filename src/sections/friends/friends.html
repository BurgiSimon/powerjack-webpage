Tell them the number.

Online is friends only, by design: no accounts, no matchmaking. One of you holds a table on your own computer and gives the others six letters and numbers, or a link. Two play. Four more can pull up a stool and watch.

You see each other as ghosts in Old Jack’s style, each in one of six tints, with your real cue, your own drinks and smoke, even your footsteps. Everyone at the table shares the same night.
