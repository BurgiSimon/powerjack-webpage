1:45 a.m. · Get down on the shot

No aim line. No power bar. Just the cue.

Walk to the table, get down on the shot, set your bridge hand and push the cue with your own mouse hand. Nothing on the screen tells you where the ball will go. Your eyes do.

“Your hand is the stroke: the faster the push, the harder the hit.”

Standing, your eye is 0.79 m over the cloth. Down on the shot, chin on the cue, it’s about 0.15. You breathe and you sway, and you’re steadier with a bridge.
