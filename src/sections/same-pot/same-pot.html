1:48 a.m. · Same shot, same pot

Five engines. One table.

“JS engines disagree in the last bit, and lockstep turns that into a different pot within a few shots.”

So the table brings its own maths. Its sine, cosine and exp are ports of FreeBSD’s msun that use only + − × ÷ and square roots, and every engine gets the same bits back. On every pull request the build plays twelve racks of twenty-five shots, with no resync, in Node, Bun, Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. That’s 1,603,498 steps, nearly 27 minutes of table time, and all five have to end on one digest.

That’s also why a shot can travel between friends as a few numbers: aim, spin, elevation, speed and squirt.

Your browser’s own Math.sin, cos and exp, over 4,096 inputs, hash to —. Open this page in a browser with a different engine and that number may change. The table’s never does.
